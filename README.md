## What is the H3 Metatype Matrix?

The **H3 Metatype Matrix** is a personality test and self-assessment tool inspired by the **Human 3.0** philosophy popularized by **Dan Koe**. Each metatype reflects a unique pattern of strengths and growth edges. The assessment uses an **AI-powered conversational approach** rather than static questionnaires, delivering a personalized radar chart and actionable development plan.

## The Four Dimensions

The Human 3.0 framework assesses four interconnected dimensions of peak performance:

### Mind
Intellectual clarity, learning capacity, strategic thinking, and cognitive discipline. A high Mind score indicates sharp analytical ability and a commitment to continuous growth.

### Body
Physical vitality, energy management, health habits, and somatic awareness. A high Body score reflects consistent investment in physical well-being as a foundation for everything else.

### Spirit
Purpose, meaning, emotional depth, inner alignment, and connection to something greater than yourself. A high Spirit score signals a strong sense of identity and values-driven living.

### Vocation
Career mastery, financial intelligence, professional impact, and the ability to create value in the world. A high Vocation score means you are building something meaningful and sustainable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is my metatype?**
Your metatype is your unique evolutionary archetype within the Human 3.0 framework. Take the [free assessment at h3test.com](https://www.h3test.com) to discover yours.

**What is the Human 3.0 framework?**
Human 3.0 is a holistic self-development philosophy that evaluates growth across four dimensions: Mind, Body, Spirit, and Vocation. It was popularized by Dan Koe as a framework for creators and entrepreneurs seeking peak performance.

**Is this a personality test for creators?**
Yes. While anyone can take it, the H3 Metatype Matrix is specifically designed for creators, entrepreneurs, solopreneurs, and self-improvers who want actionable insights — not just personality labels.

**How is this different from Myers-Briggs (MBTI)?**
MBTI focuses on cognitive preferences (introversion/extraversion, thinking/feeling). The H3 Metatype Matrix evaluates your actual life performance across Mind, Body, Spirit, and Vocation — including physical health and career, which MBTI ignores entirely.

**What are the 16 evolutionary archetypes?**
The 16 metatypes range from The Meta-Architect (high across all dimensions) to The Unawakened (low across all dimensions), with 14 unique combinations in between. Each archetype has distinct strengths, blind spots, and a growth path. See the [full table above](#the-16-metatypes).

**Is the test free?**
The core assessment, radar chart, and metatype result are completely free. A detailed 180-Day Evolutionary Roadmap with personalized action plans is available for $1.99.

**How long does the assessment take?**
The AI conversation typically takes 5–10 minutes. It adapts to your answers, so the experience is different for everyone.

**Can my metatype change over time?**
Absolutely. Your metatype reflects your current state, not a fixed identity. As you grow and develop across the four dimensions, your metatype will evolve with you.
